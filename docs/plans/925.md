# Plan: #925 volume-pool tenant moves

Objective: move a tenant's closed replica directory from its source pool
mount to its destination pool mount, node by node, behind a move journal
`__sys:volumepool/moves/{tenant}`, cut over by one assignment write
(docs/decisions/volume-pool-tenant-moves.md; Q1-Q8 and Q10 adopted, ruling
1115; Q9 decided by the owner, owner queue 287: C, refused outright).

## Slices

1. Built (sr925a). Node-step primitive, unwired. `pkg/volume/move_copy.go`:
   `CopyTree`, `BuildManifest`, `VerifyCopy`, `Manifest`, `ManifestEntry`;
   `*MoveStepError` and `*ManifestMismatchError` in `catalog_errors.go`.
   Q2 A and Q4 A. Tests: `move_copy_test.go`.

3. Built (sr925c). Journal record and catalog admit, unwired:

5a. Built (sr925f). Step transitions `GrantMoveStep`/`CompleteMoveStep`/

   `*MoveToUnencryptedPoolError` (the owner's ruling, owner queue 287),

   `move_journal_test.go`, `move_reconciler_test.go`.
5b. The node-side `MoveStepRunner` (close stores, `RunMoveCopyStep`, reopen

6a. Built (sr925h, sr925i). Cut over (`CutOverMove`, the

6b. The node-side abort and retire step runner (with 5b); the `CancelMove`

7. Composition wiring (the lifecycle replica-node source,
   `SetMoveAdmission`, `SetMoveStartAuditor`, the reconciler's leader
   schedule and transition audit), transports and CLI verbs, with CLI
   multi-transport E2E and features.mk.
   Built (sr925j): the path-leak fix in `checkRootPath`, the leader/node
   pass split (`ReconcileOnce` grants and cuts over, `RunLocalSteps(node)`
   runs and settles a node's own step), the node-side runner
   `seal.MoveStepRunner` (5b, 6b; copy reopens from the destination before
   the CAS through an in-memory override, abort settles after the CAS
   through `volume.MoveStepSettler`), `Catalog.ResolveForNode` with
   `BarrierRegistry.SetVolumeNodeID` and boot removal of an aborted
   destination, and `server.MoveStoreCloser` (the `TenantStoreCloser`,
   over the shard lifecycle and, for an IsolationVolume tenant, its Host).
   Built (sr925j, second slice): the wiring. `server.WireTenantMoves`
   builds the step runner and reconciler, sets the admission, cut-over
   recorder, start auditor and replica-node source (system-shard replica
   IDs matched by placement raft address), and returns the
   `TenantMoveWorker` (per-node `RunLocalSteps` tick plus the
   system-leader `ReconcileOnce`); `TenantInfraConfig.VolumeNodeID` sets
   the registry's volume node ID before `RestoreVolumeTenants`;
   `service.NewVolumeMoveTransitionAudit` emits
   `volume_pool.move_<transition>`. Both cmd/qrdbd/start.go and
   sdk/go/node/node.go start the worker. Tests: `move_wiring_test.go`,
   `move_store_closer_test.go`, `volume_move_audit_test.go`.
   Built (sr925k, sr925l, sr925m): the transports. `CancelMove` on
   `volume.Service` and `volumetest.Service`; `volume_pools:move`
   (cross-scope, carried by Admin); `VolumePoolMoveTenant`,
   `VolumePoolGetMove` and `VolumePoolCancelMove` on gRPC/Unix, QUIC, REST
   and MCP and the embedded transport, per the table in
   docs/decisions/volume-pool-tenant-moves.md "Transports and permission";
   the Go SDK on all six transports, the TypeScript SDK (REST; Wails
   answers not implemented), the CLI verbs `move-tenant`, `get-move` and
   `cancel-move`; the CLI E2E `volume_move_cli_test.go` in
   `INTTEST_volume_pools`; docs and CHANGELOG.
   A move completes on a running node: the copy step closes the tenant's
   shards, host, barrier and per-tenant Pebble store, copies and verifies
   the directory, and reopens everything from the destination, unsealing an
   auto-unseal tenant as boot does; while the step holds the tenant closed
   the barrier registry refuses a fresh registration, so the placement
   reconciler cannot re-register its barrier shell mid-copy.
   Built (sr925o): the reconciler hold. `MoveStoreCloser` holds the tenant
   from the start of `CloseTenantStores` until `OpenTenantStores` returns
   (a failed close or reopen releases it); `WireTenantMoves` installs it with
   `TenantShardReconciler.SetMoveHold`, and `ensureStarted` neither attaches
   a held tenant's shards nor registers its shell, so no shard is
   re-attached between the shard close and the barrier close. Tests:
   `TestMoveStoreCloser_HoldsTenantAcrossMove`,
   `TestMoveStoreCloser_FailedStepsReleaseHold`,
   `TestTenantShardReconciler_MoveHoldDefers`. Go SDK move-verb tests on
   gRPC, QUIC and REST (wire request, decoded move, not-found), beside the
   MCP and Unix ones. The E2E proves
   a completed move on every transport (data written before it reads back,
   new writes land on the destination) besides start, get, cancel and the
   refusals. A self-managed tenant comes back sealed after its copy step,
   as after a restart, until its admin unseals it. The Q9 refusal is proven
   by the handler tests only (it needs a LUKS pool).
   Known limit: a failed reopen or
   settle at runtime leaves the tenant's stores closed until the next boot.
   Known limit: on the embedded node a system-scope recycle rebuilds the
   tenant service, and the move worker keeps the one it was wired to.
## Out of scope for slices 1-6a

Composition wiring of any move seam, the node-side step runner, the
reconciler's schedule, transports, CLI, config keys. The primitive never deletes: a partial
destination is removed through `seal.BarrierRegistry.RequireInsideLocalPool`.
