# Plan: #1187 tenant delegation policy and policy-minted delegations

## Objective

A tenant holds a standing delegation policy (allowed permissions, maximum
lifetime). `Node.MintDelegation` mints a delegation only inside that
policy, and an embedder runs ordinary operations under the minted
delegation: allowed in the policy's tenant, refused in every other
tenant, refused once the policy is deleted and the serial revoked.

## Slices, in order

1. **Part 1, service and proto** (merged into this stack via #1549):
   the policy store and policy-bounded `MintDelegation`; qrdb.proto
   messages and RPCs for the delegation policy. Tests: service unit
   tests, gRPC permission and UNIMPLEMENTED-state tests.
2. **Part 2, transports and SDKs**: delegation-policy put, list and
   delete on REST, gRPC, QUIC and MCP, and on every Go SDK transport.
   Tests: handler tests per transport, SDK client tests.
3. **Part 3, CLI**: `qrdb ca delegate policy put|list|delete`.
   Tests: `cmd/qrdb` unit tests against a faithful double; CLI E2E on
   every transport, including tenant B's delete of tenant A's policy
   pinned as not-found with A's policy left standing.
4. **Part 4, embedder operations** (PR #1558): users get, roles
   get-by-name, roles resolve-permissions, roles assign/unassign, tenant
   barrier-officer list and barrier-seal under a policy-minted
   delegation. Tests: `sdk/go/node` delegated-context tests (allowed and
   audited in A, refused in B and the system tenant, no policy means no
   mint, policy delete plus serial revoke refuses the next call); CLI
   E2E of the same over REST, gRPC, QUIC, MCP and Unix.

Docs (identity-rbac and certificate-authority api, architecture, cli,
user) and CHANGELOG move with each slice.

## Out of scope

- Credential rotation policy RPCs beyond the proto messages (#1201).
- New permissions or roles; the delegation only narrows existing ones.
- Cross-tenant delegation: a policy never grants outside its tenant.
